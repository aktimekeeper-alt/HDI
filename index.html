// ABOUT HDI

Who We Are

Hyperion Defense Industries is a professional Star Citizen organization united by a common mission: to operate with the highest standard of discipline, coordination, and strategic focus in everything we do.

We bring together pilots, operators, and specialists who share a commitment to excellence. Whether executing a complex fleet engagement, managing a large-scale trade run, or coordinating critical logistics — HDI performs with precision and purpose.

From the commerce lanes of ArcCorp to the contested reaches of deep space, our members represent some of the most capable operators in the Verse. We recruit for quality, not quantity.
