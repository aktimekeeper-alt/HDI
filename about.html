// ABOUT HDI

Hyperion Defense
Industries

A professional Star Citizen organization forging excellence in every corner of the Verse — built for those who demand more from their operations.

// MISSION

Our Mission

"To build a professional, coordinated organization that delivers strength in combat and opportunity in enterprise — for the benefit of our members and the communities we serve across the United Earth Empire."
// WHO WE ARE

Built from the Ground Up

Hyperion Defense Industries was founded on a straightforward premise: the best operations require the best people, working with the best tools, under the best leadership.

HDI isn't just another org — it's a structured, goal-oriented organization with clear chains of command, defined operational roles, and a culture that rewards dedication and professionalism. We don't just play the game; we master it.

Based out of Area 18 on ArcCorp in the Stanton system, HDI operates across UEE space with reach into contested and unexplored regions. Our members span time zones and backgrounds, united by a shared standard of excellence.

// WHAT DRIVES US

The HDI Standard

We measure success not just by missions accomplished, but by how we accomplish them — with integrity, precision, and respect for every member of the fleet.

New recruits enter a structured onboarding process that sets expectations clearly and connects them with experienced operators immediately. From your first day, you're part of something real.

Leadership is earned, not given. We reward proven operators with greater responsibility and opportunity, building the next generation of HDI commanders from within our own ranks.

// VALUES

Core Values

These principles define how we operate — in every mission, every interaction, and every decision.

Professionalism

We hold ourselves to a high standard in all interactions. Discipline, respect, and accountability are non-negotiable, in combat and out.

Brotherhood

We look out for each other. HDI is a community, not just an org. Our people come first, and we invest in every member's success.

Coordination

Strength comes from unity. Our operations succeed because we communicate, plan, and execute together — no solo runs, no gaps in the line.

Growth

We are always improving — as individuals and as an organization. Continuous learning, skill development, and adaptation define HDI.

Precision

We execute with clarity of purpose. Every operation is planned, every role is assigned, and every outcome is measured against our goals.

Integrity

Our word is our bond. Agreements with allies, contracts for clients, and commitments to our own members are honored without exception.
